# Detailed Case Study

## Objective

Organize a safe, enjoyable, and well-coordinated one-day team outing that remains within budget and meets participant expectations.

## Requirements Management

The requirements baseline contains five stakeholder requirements. Three are high priority, one is medium priority, and one is low priority. The low-priority dog-friendly requirement was rejected because of park rules.

| ID | Description | Source | Priority | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-01 | Cost under $50 per person | Management | High | Approved | Core constraint |
| REQ-02 | Suitable for various fitness levels | Team Survey | High | Approved | Selected moderate trail |
| REQ-03 | Include a meal (picnic-style) | Team Survey | High | Approved | BBQ plan created |
| REQ-04 | Location within 1-hour drive | Team Survey | Medium | Approved | Maple Creek Park chosen |
| REQ-05 | Dog-friendly activity | Several Members | Low | Rejected | Park rules prohibit pets |

## Project Planning

The project uses a $440 planned budget and finishes at $400 actual expenditure, producing a positive variance of $40.

| Category | Planned | Actual | Variance | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Transportation | $120 | $120 | $0 | On Track |
| Food & Drinks | $200 | $185 | +$15 | Under Budget |
| Park Fees | $50 | $50 | $0 | On Track |
| Supplies | $30 | $35 | −$5 | Slight Overrun |
| Contingency | $40 | $10 | +$30 | Under Budget |

## Monitoring and Control

The status dashboard tracks timeline adherence, budget performance, participation, and risk handling. The purpose is visibility: compare actual project behavior with the baseline and act when deviations emerge.

## Quality Assurance

The audit records one non-conformance in food safety. Corrective action was immediate: the affected potato salad was discarded. The remaining checkpoints passed.

Quality scores:

- Planning: 95%
- Execution: 85%
- Closure: 100%
- Overall quality: 93%
